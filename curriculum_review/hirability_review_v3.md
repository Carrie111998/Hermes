## Coursera Course Deep-Dive (Why These 3)

### 1. Machine Learning Engineering for Production (MLOps)
**Partner:** DeepLearning.AI (Andrew Ng)
**Why:** Industry gold standard. Covers CI/CD for ML, deployment pipelines, monitoring, data drift. Not beginner — assumes you can build models. This makes you a **production engineer**, not just a builder.
**Singapore relevance:** GovTech, DBS, OCBC all hiring for MLOps. This is the #1 skill gap.

### 2. AI Agents and MLOps for Production-Ready AI
**Partner:** Packt
**Why:** Directly about agentic workflows, LangGraph, CrewAI, containerization. Most applied course for your current work. Pivots you from "basic RAG" to "robust multi-agent orchestration."
**Singapore relevance:** Smart Nation initiatives need people who can productionize agent systems.

### 3. MLOps and Responsible AI Practices
**Partner:** Microsoft
**Why:** Covers data ethics, model governance, compliance — maps directly to Singapore's **AI Verify** framework. Essential for regulated sectors (finance, government).
**Singapore relevance:** Banks and government agencies won't deploy AI without governance. This is a differentiator.
