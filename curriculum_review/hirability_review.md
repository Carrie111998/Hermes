## Executive Summary
The current curriculum is a solid foundation but **over-indexed on academic/introductory material** for someone already building with MCP and agents. To become "hyper-hirable" in Singapore, Chin must pivot from "How do I build an agent?" to "How do I ship production-grade, secure, multi-agent systems that solve business problems?"

### Rationale
*   **Phase 1 (Master Agent/MCP):** Excellent. Direct application of skills already in use.
*   **Phase 2 (Vanderbilt/Coursera):** Pivot. These courses are too general for a developer who already has a functional agent. They are "resume fillers," not skill-builders. Replace with advanced systems architecture (latency, cost management, data privacy in LLMs).
*   **Phase 3 (Packt/General Build):** Pivot. Don't read books on "how to build agents" — build an agent that integrates a high-value Singaporean API (e.g., SingPass, MAS financial APIs, or complex ERP connectors).

---

## Urgent Skill Gaps (Singapore Market)
The Singapore market is currently obsessed with **Operationalization (MLOps)** and **Governance (AI TRAC)**.

1.  **AI Governance & Compliance:** Familiarity with Singapore’s Model AI Governance Framework and AI Verify (AIV). Employers value candidates who understand the *ethics/risk* side of deployment.
2.  **Performance Engineering:** Scaling agents. How to optimize prompt caching, latency, and token cost for high-traffic environments.
3.  **Data Privacy:** Building agents that interact with sensitive PII in compliance with PDPA.
4.  **Integration Logic:** Moving beyond basic RAG to complex tool-calling workflows that involve multi-step authentication (OIDC/OAuth2 flows).

---

## Final Recommendation
1.  **Drop** the general Coursera/Packt theory courses.
2.  **Replace** with a targeted project: *An agent that automates a complex, multi-step business workflow (e.g., procurement or financial reporting) incorporating a security/governance review layer.*
3.  **Content Strategy:** Document the *challenges* (latency/costs/security) on LinkedIn, not just the success. This signals seniority.

**Overall Assessment:** If Chin follows the original plan, he remains a "builder." If he follows the revised, governance-and-scale-focused plan, he becomes an "AI Systems Architect." The latter is significantly more hireable in Singapore.
