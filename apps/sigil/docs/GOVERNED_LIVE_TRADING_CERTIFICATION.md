# Governed Live Trading Certification

Sigil Step 29 converts an approved Step 28 eligibility review into a narrowly
scoped live-trading certification artifact.

The certification binds:

- broker and account identity
- allowed asset classes
- allowed order types
- allowed symbols
- initial capital and per-order ceilings
- validity start and expiration
- kill-switch and rollback references
- immutable evidence
- certifier identity

Certification states are `denied`, `certified`, `revoked`, and `expired`.

This milestone does not connect to a broker or place trades. Certification is a
governed prerequisite for later launch controls, not unrestricted authority.
