# Governed Paper Trading Promotion Readiness

Sigil Step 27 aggregates multiple Step 26 performance evaluations and determines
whether the accumulated paper-trading record is ready for a future governed
promotion decision.

The assessment produces `not_ready`, `review_required`, or `paper_ready`.

It enforces minimum evaluation history, passed evaluations, pass rate, total
paper P/L, average compliance, failed/review limits, unique sessions, and
complete evidence.

`paper_ready` does not authorize live trading. It means only that the configured
paper-history requirements have passed and may proceed to another governed
review stage.
