# Governed Live Trading Eligibility Review

Sigil Step 28 reviews whether a paper-ready trading record may proceed to a
future live-trading certification stage.

The review verifies paper readiness, freshness, explicit operator approval,
broker and account identity, credential attestation, legal acknowledgment,
jurisdiction, initial capital limits, per-order limits, kill-switch coverage,
rollback planning, unresolved blockers, and evidence.

The review produces:

- `ineligible`
- `conditional`
- `eligible_for_live_certification`

Eligibility is not authorization to trade. This milestone does not connect to a
broker, expose credentials, or place orders.
