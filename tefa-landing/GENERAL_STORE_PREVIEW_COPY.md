# General Store preview copy contract

Until payment processing, tax, shipping, product data, order records, and fulfillment are production-ready, public labels must use:

- **General Store Preview**
- **Preview cart**
- **Checkout preview**
- **Add to preview cart**
- **Preview confirmation**
- **Preview complete — no order placed**

Do not use "real checkout," "pay," or "place order" in the prototype interface. Keep General Store outside the public ESA buyer path.
