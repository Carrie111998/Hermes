What these tools build

School covers the basics. We stock everything after that.

A young person can pass every class and still not know what to do when a situation turns, when they're the only one in the room who thinks something is wrong, or when they have a skill and no idea what it's worth.

That's the gap we supply. Not another curriculum — equipment, kits, and guided projects a student works through and keeps. What they build is capability they can point at, and the confidence that comes with it, which is a far better defence against bad influence than being told to make good choices.

Handling it

Knowing what to do when something goes sideways

Conflict, pressure, authority, emergencies, being blamed, being wrong. Rehearsed before it counts, so the first time isn't the real time.

Self-command

Running yourself when nobody is checking

Attention, follow-through, and the discipline to finish a build that stopped being fun in the middle. The habit transfers everywhere.

Channelled energy

Somewhere to put it

Restlessness isn't a defect, it's unspent fuel. Every kit gives it a direction and a finish line — hours that would otherwise go looking for something to do.

Adding value

Being the person who can do the thing

Fixing what's broken, designing what's needed, shipping what was promised — and knowing how to price it. Value you create is leverage nobody can take.

Practical skill

Hands that know things

Tools, materials, measurement, machines, software. Competence with real objects, which is still the fastest way to stop feeling powerless.

Questions families ask
Do you only work with Texas families?

No — we ship to all 50 states. What varies is the funding: TEFA is Texas's program, and other states run their own ESA, voucher, or school-choice programs with separate vendor registration and different rules about what counts as an eligible expense. The programs we're currently registered with are listed in the funding section. If yours isn't there, ask — we'll tell you honestly whether we can serve it.

Are you a school, a tutor, or a store?

A store. We supply tools, kits, and equipment along with the project books that make them worth owning. We don't enrol students, run classes, or issue grades — which also means your funds go toward materials your student keeps rather than hours that end.

I don't have an education savings account yet. Can you help?

Yes, and it's free of the usual runaround — see funding assistance. We identify your state's program, check eligibility with you, walk the application through line by line, and set up your first order so it clears. What we can't do is approve anything: the awarding decision belongs entirely to the agency running your program, and any vendor who promises you an award is one to avoid.

How do I know an item is eligible before I buy it?

Ask us first, always. Categories differ by program and change between years. Send your state and the SKUs and we'll confirm in writing what your funds cover — including when the answer is that they don't cover something, which happens and is better learned before the invoice than after.

Why is there no checkout button?

Because education funds pay against an approved invoice rather than a card. Adding a cart would just create a payment you'd have to reverse. We quote, your program approves, we ship — and you never front the money.

Can I buy without using program funds?

[Yes / no — state your direct-purchase terms and how to pay.] Plenty of families want a kit outside their allocation or don't qualify for a program at all, so it's worth answering this one clearly.

What ages are these for?

Broadly 8–18, graded by readiness rather than grade level. The tool roll and the electronics bench assume supervision at the younger end; the design, motion, and skill-to-income kits land best from about 13 up. Tell us the student and we'll steer you.

What's your returns policy?

[State it plainly — e.g. unopened kits returnable within 30 days; consumables and opened software non-returnable; defective items replaced free.] Families spending restricted funds reasonably want this in writing before they order, and some programs require a published policy on file.

How can I verify the carbon spending you advertise?

We publish a [quarterly / annual] summary of what was committed and where it went, including the partner we purchased through. Ask for the most recent one before you order — we'd rather you check.
