About us — our mission

Competence is the best protection a kid can carry.

Most young people aren't short on energy or intelligence. They're short on places to put it. A student who can fix the thing, budget the money, hold the plan, and read the room is far harder to knock off course than one who has only been told to behave.

So we don't lead with lectures. Every module ends with something the student can demonstrate — a repaired object, a working budget, a project they built, a claim they checked and disproved. Confidence follows capability. It rarely works the other way around.

We're a supplement, not a replacement. School covers the academics. We cover the practical ground it doesn't have hours for, and we do it in a way that gives your family something concrete to show for the funds you spent.

Skill

Something they can actually do

Hands-on work with a finished result. Tools, money, food, time, repairs, and the small competencies that quietly make an adult useful.

Self-command

Energy pointed somewhere

Goal-setting that survives a bad week, habits that hold when nobody is watching, and a plan for the hours that would otherwise go looking for trouble.

Judgment

The habit of asking who benefits

Students practise spotting manipulation — in a feed, an ad, a generated image, or a friend's bad idea. Scepticism they can aim, so pressure loses its grip.

For parents

You should know exactly what your child is doing with AI.

Handing a young person an open chatbot and hoping for the best isn't education, and it isn't safe. Our AI track is supervised, bounded, and built to make the student the one asking the hard questions.

  • Accounts are set up with you, not around you — you hold the credentials.
  • No open-ended, unsupervised chatbot time. Every session has a task and an end.
  • Work is logged and shareable, so you can read exactly what was asked and answered.
  • Assignments start offline. Students think first, then check their thinking against a model.
  • We teach where AI is confidently wrong, and how to catch it, before we teach convenience.
  • Nothing personal about your child goes into a public tool. That rule is taught and enforced.
