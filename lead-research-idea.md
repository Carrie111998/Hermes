lead research is the most vital feature of the current agent structure.


it should work effectively and provide pinpoint leads to the users depending on the market and the scoring system user determined.

workflow should go like this:

user selects area/country/countries to make the lead research

user determines the scoring system (based on what criteria) how important each of the criteria is (e.g. import volume is 10% of the score, number of employees is 5% of the score, etc.) this feature is dynamic on webui so every user can modify their own criteria.

the system scans the data sources it has for lead connection, after it finds a potential lead it scores it based on the criteria and user's settings, and shows the lead to the user with the score.

the scoring is made on the agentic research of the leads. which is made once and saved to the database with the research results.

but if the lead is never researched agents does a deep dive to gather information and validate the facts we have about them.

after finding a potential lead company users will have the option to research the lead contacts of the founded lead company.

how contact reach works:
- if we have a mail adress in database we use it.
- we find higher position people on the company via linkedin or other sources then match the mail structure to their to find their email adress. after determining we try to validate and showcase the user with label. (green, yellow, red) corresponding to reliability of the found email adress.
- after that we complete the research of contacts.


at the end we provide users with leads, contacts and other resources they might need to start a business relationship with the lead. score we gave them based on their criteria and user determined scoring system. as well as the found contacts and verification states. 


all saved to our client's lead list. which they will be able to use in email and whatsapp reach campaigns later. all of this data is visable by the admin aswell.
