# Neon Genie — Premiere thesis

**The Hermes skill for governed opportunity and product intelligence.**

Not more ideas. **Better decisions under uncertainty.**

---

## What “premiere of its type” means

| Goal | Neon Genie wins when… |
|------|------------------------|
| **Trust density** | Operators trust it more than idea-agents because it refuses fantasy, labels claims, and fails closed |
| **Outcome density** | Packets define externally checkable proof and survive contact with reality |
| **Category ownership** | “Use neon-genie for X” is the default Hermes phrase for governed invention / product intent |

**Conflict rule:** Find public facts → **request** private facts → only then `NOT_COMPUTABLE`. Never invent `OBSERVED`.

**Priority stack:** Evidence-seeking honesty → Outcomes → Category.

---

## Why not generic product chat / “startup GPT”?

| Typical idea agent | Neon Genie |
|--------------------|------------|
| Invents freely; sounds confident | Invents under gates; sounds careful |
| Mixes user, buyer, payer | Separates roles or fails Gate C |
| Prices and market sizes without sources | Labels `SPECULATIVE` / `NOT_COMPUTABLE` |
| Skips missing private data | Emits `DataRequest` |
| “Ship it” energy | **Advisory only** — no spend/publish/execute |
| Unstable handoffs to eng | Wayfinder packets freeze product intent |

Neon Genie is optimized to be **harder to impress** and **more decision-useful**, not louder.

---

## Category definition

| Neon Genie **is** | Neon Genie **is not** |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Hermes-native skill for opportunity & product intelligence | Kubrick (cinematic / symbolic dramaturgy) |
| Evidence-bound invention + product architecture | Wayfinder (work decomposition / eng execution) |
| Fail-closed, claim-labeled, handoff-ready | A Python product-reasoning engine |
| Find / request / refuse | Auto-spend, auto-outreach, canon promotion |

Sibling of **Kubrick** (shared packaging architecture). Handoff peer of **Wayfinder** (intent vs implementation).

---

## Signature loop

```text
OPEN → ALIGN → ASCEND → CLEAR → SEAL
```

With:

1. **Research** — proactive when public facts improve the decision  
2. **DataRequest** — when private facts block the decision  
3. **completion_proof** — what the world must show for success  
4. **Learning ledger** — PROPOSED observations only after real contact
